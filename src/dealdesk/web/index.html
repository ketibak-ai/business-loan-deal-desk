Borrower's Deal Desk

A business loan calculator that also estimates how the bank prices your deal, so you can see how much room there is to negotiate before you sign.

Example numbers. This is a made-up business (Cedar & Pine Millwork) with a made-up offer. Replace them with your own. Market rates below are examples too, so update them to today's rates. Your inputs are saved only in this browser.

For education and planning only. Not a loan offer, credit decision or financial advice. The bank's view is an estimate built from common industry methods: a 12% RAROC hurdle, Basel IRB capital and funds transfer pricing. Every lender's model, policy and appetite are different. Check SBA program limits and fees at sba.gov. Source, REST API and method: github.com/ketibak-ai/business-loan-deal-desk.
