# Term Sheet Glossary

## Interest Rate and APR
The interest rate is the charge on the loan balance. The annual percentage rate (APR) adds upfront and ongoing fees to show the true annual cost of borrowing. Two loans with the same rate can have very different APRs. Always compare offers by APR.

## Index and Spread
A floating rate is an index plus a spread. The index, such as SOFR or Prime, moves with the market. The spread, quoted in basis points, is fixed for the life of the loan and is the part you negotiate. A rate floor sets a minimum for the index.

## Basis Points
A basis point (bp) is one hundredth of one percent. 100 bps equals 1%. On a $1 million loan, 25 bps is $2,500 of interest a year.

## Amortization, Term and Balloon
Amortization is the schedule that sets the payment amount. The term is when the loan must be repaid. When amortization is longer than the term, a balloon payment of the remaining balance is due at maturity.

## Debt Service Coverage Ratio
Debt service coverage ratio (DSCR) is cash flow available for debt service, often EBITDA, divided by total annual principal and interest payments. Banks typically require 1.25x, meaning $1.25 of cash flow for every $1 of payments.

## Leverage
Leverage is total debt divided by EBITDA. Most banks become cautious above 3.0x to 3.5x for small and mid-size businesses.

## Loan to Value
Loan to value (LTV) is the loan amount divided by the appraised value of the collateral. Lower LTV means more collateral cushion for the lender and usually better pricing.

## Unused Fee
On a line of credit, the unused fee is charged on the committed but undrawn amount, quoted in basis points a year.

## Compensating Balance
A compensating balance is a minimum deposit the bank requires you to keep as a loan condition. It raises your effective borrowing cost if the balance earns less than you could earn elsewhere.
